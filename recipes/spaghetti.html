Spaghetti

Description

A sweet, rich, and meaty pasta dish traditionally served at birthdays and family gatherings.

Ingredients
  • 2 lbs (approx. 900g) dried spaghetti noodles
  • 1 lb ground pork or ground beef (or a mix of both)
  • 4 to 6 sliced red Filipino-style hot dogs or beef franks
  • 6 oz minced luncheon meat (optional)
  • 1 pack (35 oz / 900g) Filipino-style sweet spaghetti sauce
  • 1/2 to 1 cup banana ketchup (for the signature tang and color)
  • 1 cup beef or chicken broth
  • 1 onion and 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 to 1/2 cup evaporated milk or a dash of condensed milk (for creaminess)
  • Salt, pepper, and sugar to taste
  • Grated cheddar cheese or processed cheese (such as Eden) for topping
Instructions
  1. Cook the noodles: Boil a large pot of salted water and cook the spaghetti according to package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. Brown the hot dogs: In a large pan or Dutch oven, heat a little oil and sear the sliced hot dogs until lightly browned. Remove and set aside.
  3. Sauté aromatics and meat: In the same pan, sauté the minced garlic and onions until soft. Add the ground pork or beef and cook until browned.
  4. Build the sauce: Stir in the tomato paste, Filipino-style spaghetti sauce, banana ketchup, and beef broth. Add the cooked hot dogs back in.
  5. Simmer: Cover and simmer over low-to-medium heat for 20–30 minutes. Stir in the milk and adjust sweetness with a little sugar, salt, and pepper.
  6. Combine or serve: Toss the cooked noodles directly into the sauce or plate the pasta individually, ladle the rich meat sauce generously on top, and finish with a heavy heap of shredded cheddar cheese.
